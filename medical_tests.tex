% Preface
\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
\markboth{Preface}{Preface}

Modern clinical practice relies heavily on diagnostic testing. This book aims to bridge the gap between test ordering and result interpretation.

\textbf{Organization:} This book covers medical laboratory tests organized alphabetically by test name. Individual tests are presented in a standardized format with 10 sections: What Is This Test?, Alternative Names, Principle, History, Why This Test Is Ordered, Primary vs Secondary Test, How This Test Is Performed, What Preparation Is Needed, Contraindications and Precautions, Risks, What Happens After the Test, Understanding Results (Below Normal / Above Normal), Normal Results, and Follow-up Tests.
